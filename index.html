Size guide

Find your binder size

Two measurements, two minutes. Use a soft tape measure and keep it level and snug, not tight.

How to measure

  1. Chest: measure around the fullest part of your chest, under your arms, while breathing normally.
  2. Shoulders: measure across your back, from the edge of one shoulder to the other.
  3. Between sizes? Size up. A binder that's too tight isn't flatter, just riskier.

How do I find my size?

Binding is safer when you wear the right size, keep to 8 hours a day at most, never sleep or do intense exercise in it, and take it off if you feel pain or can't breathe easily.
